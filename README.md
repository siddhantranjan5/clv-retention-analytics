# 📊 Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) Based Retention Framework

## 🚀 Project Overview
This project develops a data-driven Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) framework to identify high-value customers and design targeted retention strategies.

The objective was not just to calculate CLV, but to translate customer analytics into actionable business decisions that reduce churn risk and protect revenue.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem
Businesses often treat all customers equally, resulting in inefficient marketing spend and unnoticed revenue leakage from high-value customers.

This project answers:
- Which customers contribute the most long-term value?
- Which high-value customers are at risk of churn?
- How can retention efforts be optimized using data?

---

## 💼 Business Impact
- Protected approximately ₹0.85M in potential revenue
- Identified high-value, high-risk customer segments
- Designed CLV-based retention targeting strategy
- Improved decision-making for marketing allocation

## 📈 Methodology

### Data Cleaning & Preparation
- Removed missing and duplicate records
- Treated anomalies in transaction values
- Structured customer-level dataset

### Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Revenue distribution analysis
- Purchase frequency trends
- Customer segmentation patterns

### RFM Analysis
- Recency
- Frequency
- Monetary value scoring

### CLV Calculation
- Estimated customer lifetime value
- Ranked customers based on long-term contribution

### Risk & Retention Framework
- Identified high-value customers with declining activity
- Proposed targeted retention interventions
- Simulated revenue risk exposure

---

## 📊 Key Insights

- ~20% of customers contributed ~70–80% of total revenue (Pareto concentration observed).
- A subset of high-CLV customers showed declining recency scores — indicating churn risk.
- Revenue exposure was concentrated among a small segment, increasing volatility risk.
- Targeted retention for top-tier customers delivers higher ROI than blanket campaigns.


---

## 🧠 Strategic Takeaway

Customer analytics is most powerful when tied to revenue protection.

Rather than predicting churn generically, this framework prioritizes customers 
based on lifetime value and business impact — ensuring retention budgets are 
allocated where they matter most.
